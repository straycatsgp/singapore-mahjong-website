How It Plays

Closer to a 3D version of a block-fitting puzzle than a falling-block game — you're always in control of where a piece lands.

🧊

Place, Don't Just Drop

Pieces don't fall on their own. Rotate them on any axis and slide them into place by hand, then watch the well fill up from the bottom — one careless gap can come back to haunt you.

🎯

Clear Layers, Beat the Clock

Fill a full horizontal layer to clear it and score a time-based bonus — the faster you clear it, the bigger the reward. Miss a full layer clear-up and that gap gets buried for good.

💣

Bomb & Swap Power-Ups

Earn Bomb and Swap charges every time you clear a layer, with no cap on how many you can bank. Bomb blasts away a bad placement; Swap rerolls the piece in your hand — both come in clutch when a run gets ambitious.

🎚️

Three Difficulty Levels

Easy, Normal and Difficult each tune the pad size, the piece variety, the countdown, and the scoring — so the challenge scales with you, not just the clock.

👻

Optional Landing Guide

A ghost outline shows exactly where a piece will land. Turn it off for a harder, higher-scoring run — every point earned without it counts double.

🎵

Your Sound, Your Way

Independent Music and Sound Effects toggles, so you can play with the full soundtrack, sound effects only, or in total silence.
