Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 September 2026  •  Developer: Yvo Goossens / Gooseberry Studio  •  App: BlockWell (iOS, iPadOS)

BlockWell itself does not collect any personal data directly. The app shows non-personalized ads and offers a Game Center leaderboard, both of which involve some data collection by Google and Apple as described below - BlockWell never sees, stores, or shares that data itself.

1. Information We Collect

The following preferences and progress are saved locally on your device only:

  • Your selected difficulty level (Easy, Normal, or Difficult)
  • Whether the landing guide is shown
  • Whether background music and sound effects are enabled
  • Your best score reached on this device

These values are stored using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults) and never leave your device.

BlockWell also includes non-personalized advertising (Google AdMob), a leaderboard (Apple Game Center), and an optional "Remove Ads" purchase (Apple's App Store / StoreKit). Each of these involves data collection by Google or Apple, not by us directly - see the next sections for what each one collects.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

BlockWell shows ads through Google AdMob, configured to serve only non-personalized ads - ads are chosen contextually, not based on your activity in other apps or websites, and we do not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because we do not request or use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking.

To select, serve, and measure these ads, Google's SDK collects:

  • A device identifier (not the IDFA, since we don't request it)
  • Your approximate (coarse) location, estimated from your IP address
  • Which ads were shown and basic interaction data (e.g., taps)
  • Crash and performance diagnostics from the ad SDK itself

This data is handled by Google, not by us. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's AdMob data disclosure for details. Purchasing "Remove Ads" (see Section 4) stops all ad requests, and none of this data is collected afterward.

3. Game Center Leaderboard

If you're signed in to Game Center, BlockWell submits your score at the end of each run to power the built-in leaderboard. This associates your score with your Game Center player ID, entirely through Apple's Game Center service - we don't operate any server of our own and never see this data ourselves. See Apple's Game Center privacy information for details on how Apple handles this.

4. In-App Purchase (Remove Ads)

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We never see or receive your payment details, billing address, or any other purchase information - Apple only tells the app whether the purchase was completed.

5. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any separate analytics or crash-reporting service of our own, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The only data collection beyond on-device storage is what's described above for AdMob (non-personalized only) and Game Center.

6. Children's Privacy

BlockWell is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because ads are shown through Google AdMob, families using this app should also review Google's own guidance on child-directed treatment and applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA) in their region. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details in Section 10 and we will address it.

7. Data Security

Settings stored locally on your device are subject to the security protections provided by Apple's iOS and iPadOS operating systems. Data collected by Google (for ads) or Apple (for Game Center and purchases) is protected under their own respective security practices, linked above.

8. Your Rights

We ourselves do not hold any personal data - so there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our end. If you wish to clear your local settings and best score, you can delete and reinstall the app, which removes all locally stored data. To exercise rights over data held by Google or Apple directly (such as your advertising or Game Center data), use the privacy controls on your device (Settings app) or contact Google/Apple directly through the links in Sections 2 and 3.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
