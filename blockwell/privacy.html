Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 September 2026  •  Developer: Yvo Goossens / Gooseberry Studio  •  App: BlockWell (iOS, iPadOS)

BlockWell does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

BlockWell does not collect any personal information. The app operates entirely on-device and makes no network connections.

The following preferences and progress are saved locally on your device only:

  • Your selected difficulty level (Easy, Normal, or Difficult)
  • Whether the landing guide is shown
  • Whether background music and sound effects are enabled
  • Your best score reached on this device

These values are stored using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). They never leave your device.

2. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any analytics services, crash-reporting tools, advertising networks, or third-party tracking SDKs. No data about your gameplay, device, or behaviour is collected or reported anywhere.

3. Third-Party Services

BlockWell does not integrate any third-party services, frameworks, or libraries that collect data. The app does not make any network requests.

4. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is collected at all, the app is safe for all ages in this regard.

5. Data Security

Because no personal data is transmitted, there is no data in transit to protect. Settings stored locally on your device are subject to the security protections provided by Apple's iOS and iPadOS operating systems.

6. Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected or stored by us, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our end. If you wish to clear your local settings and best score, you can delete and reinstall the app, which removes all locally stored data.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
